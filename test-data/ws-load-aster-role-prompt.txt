Company facts

2. The company was founded in 2019. Its headquarters are in the fictional city of Portmere.

14. A critical incident that remains unresolved for 30 minutes must be escalated to the Duty Operations Manager.
15. During an active delivery delay, the customer receives an updated estimated arrival time every 20 minutes.
16. A planned route change must be requested at least 48 hours before collection and approved by the Routing Coordinator.
